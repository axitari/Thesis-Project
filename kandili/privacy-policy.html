Privacy Policy & Terms

Kandili Workplace Load and Wellness System

1. Overview

Kandili is designed to help DepEd schools monitor workload, employee wellness, and performance-related indicators. By registering and using this system, users agree to the collection, use, and protection of their information as described below.

2. Privacy Policy

We collect personal and work-related information necessary for account access and organizational reporting, including:

  • Name, email address, and contact details
  • Employment details such as employee ID, school assignment, and department
  • Workload, wellness, and assessment information relevant to reporting and monitoring

This information is used to manage system access, generate workload and burnout reports, support school planning, and improve employee well-being tracking. We do not sell or share personal information with unauthorized third parties.

3. Data Security

Kandili uses secure authentication and stores user data in a protected environment. Access to sensitive data is limited to authorized users such as administrators, principals, and designated school personnel according to their roles.

4. Terms & Conditions

Users are responsible for providing accurate information, maintaining the confidentiality of their login credentials, and using the platform only for legitimate school functions. Any misuse of the system, unauthorized access, or manipulation of records may result in restricted access or disciplinary action.

  • Users must provide truthful employment and school-related information.
  • Users must not share personal login credentials with others.
  • Reports and data generated by the system are intended for authorized school use.
  • The platform may be updated periodically to improve performance, compliance, and user experience.

5. Consent

By creating an account and continuing to use Kandili, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to this Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.
